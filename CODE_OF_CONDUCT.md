# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We commit to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume positive intent.
- Provide actionable feedback.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or public shaming.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, or PRs that violate this policy.
